## What is Tarot reading?

[Tarot reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarot_card_reading) is a form of fortune telling involving a special deck of playing cards with evocative illustrations ([here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rider%E2%80%93Waite_Tarot) is the most famous set).

The deck involves four suits called wands, cups, swords and pentacles (these are alternative names for clubs, hearts, spades and diamonds) and 22 extra face cards called "major arcana". 

In a typical reading, a questioner, possibly with help from a reader, thinks of a question, chooses some cards then arranges them face down in a pattern called a "spread", with the positions in the pattern indicating some meaning. For example, a three card spread might represent the past, present and future. They then turn the cards over and use them as a prompt to think or talk about the answer to their question.

There are no rules for how to interpret the cards, but there is no shortage of starting points. First, there is a lot going on in the pictures: you can just ask how the things in the picture make you feel and how they relate to your question and spread. For more inspiration there is a large amount of material online about the cards' many-layered mystical meanings: [this card list](https://www.dailytarotdraw.com/tarot-card-list) is a good starting point. You may want to use the convention that a card's orientation (i.e. whether it is right-way-up or upside-down) indicates whether to focus on good or bad connotations.

## How to do the exercise

First, find a partner and decide who will be the questioner and who will be the reader.

Then decide a question to discuss. Any question works: open-ended questions leave a lot of room for interpretation, but it can also be fun to ask extremely specific ones! [Here](https://www.sherylwagnermedium.com/blog/questions-to-ask-tarot-cards)  are some [lists](https://www.tarotmasterguide.com/blogs/tarot-insights/what-to-ask-the-tarot-50-powerful-questions-for-self-discovery) of [questions](https://forum.thetarot.guru/t/49-of-the-best-tarot-questions/521) for inspiration.

Now choose a spread. Feel free to [research](https://www.tarotnova.app/blog/most-used-tarot-spreads), but in my opinion the three card [past-present-future](https://daily-tarot-girl.com/tarot-card-spreads/3-card-tarot-spread-past-present-future/) spread is a good default.

Now conduct the reading. Lay out the cards face down in your chosen spread, then turn them over one by one and discuss the answer to your question as it emerges! The only twist on a standard Tarot reading is that, as well as its other meanings and connotations, in this exercise each card represents a learning concept, which you can find on the page for the exercise. Try to incorporate the concepts into your discussion: it's fine to look them up on the internet.
