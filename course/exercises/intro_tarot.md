# Exercise: General Bayesian statistics Tarot

The first Tarot reading of the course: form pairs and answer at least one question with the help of the Tarot deck, taking into account the meanings listed below.

See [the introduction](tarot.md) for more about Tarot reading and how to do these exercises.

## Suit cards
Pentacles: Measurement
Swords: Information
Cups: Variation
Wands: Computation

Numbers mean amounts: for example, we might interpret the five of pentacles as representing five measurements. What would you do with five measurements?

Face cards mean: 
- Page = zero
- Knight = more
- Queen = the right amount
- King = infinity
